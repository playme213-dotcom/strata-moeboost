Measured on the host. The GPU's time is what passes between the host's hand-offs: from the plan to the CPU's rows it computes the experts in VRAM (and may wait for the rows), after the rows it combines and runs the next layer's attention. Double-click or scroll to zoom (shift + double-click zooms out), drag to pan: that stops the scroll, and Live follows the engine again.
